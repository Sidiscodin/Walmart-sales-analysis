## Introduction

This project focuses on **data-driven decision making** by analyzing Walmart’s historical sales dataset.  
Through **Python-based EDA** and **SQL analytics**, the project answers key business questions such as:

- Which stores and departments generate the most revenue?
- How do seasonal trends affect sales?
- What impact do holidays have on performance?
- How can inventory and promotions be optimized?

---

## Objectives

- Clean and preprocess Walmart sales data for analysis.
- Perform EDA to detect patterns, trends, and anomalies.
- Use SQL queries to answer targeted business questions.
- Visualize findings using charts and graphs for better decision-making.

---

## Features

- **Data Cleaning**: Handling missing values, duplicates, and formatting.
- **Python EDA**: Statistical summaries, trend analysis, seasonal patterns.
- **SQL Analytics**: Writing optimized queries for business KPIs.
- **Visualization**: Line charts, bar graphs, box plots for better insights.
- **Business Insights**: Actionable recommendations for sales improvement.

## Dataset

The dataset contains Walmart sales records across multiple stores and departments.  
**Key columns include**:

- `Store` – Store ID
- `Dept` – Department number
- `Date` – Sales date
- `Weekly_Sales` – Sales amount for the week
- `Holiday_Flag` – Indicates if the week includes a holiday
- `Temperature`, `Fuel_Price`, `CPI`, `Unemployment` – External factors affecting sales
